How the numbers are built

  • No job portal has an "AI-enabled BI developer" title. This profile is built from AmbitionBox BI roles whose work takes in Fabric, Copilot or AI-assisted analytics: Senior Power BI Developer, Senior BI Developer, BI Engineer, Power BI Consultant, Analytics Engineer and Power Platform Developer. Core BI means Power BI Developer and BI Developer.
  • AI Engineer and Azure Data Engineer pay are left out on purpose. AI engineers build and train AI models, and Azure data engineers build data pipelines. Both are different jobs from using AI inside BI development, so neither is a fair comparison here.
  • For 5 years 7 months, each role's low and high are read a third of the way from its 3–6 year band (centred at 4.5 years) to its 6–9 year band (centred at 7.5 years). The minimum is the lowest low and the maximum the highest high among roles that meet three conditions: at least 10 salaries in the 3–6 year band, at least 5 reported salaries in the 6–9 year band, and AmbitionBox data updated within the last six months. Older or thinner figures still appear in the drill-down, marked as not counted. The typical figure is the salary-weighted midpoint.
  • All figures are annual fixed CTC in ₹ lakh, as reported by employees on AmbitionBox (updated Oct–Nov 2026). Job postings come from Naukri searches for Microsoft Fabric, Power BI Copilot, Power BI generative AI and Power BI AI/ML, read on 2 Nov 2026. Only postings that show pay are included.
  • Outside surveys put AI-skill pay premiums at 20–56%, but they are not specific to Power BI or Fabric, so they are not used in any figure here.
  • Glassdoor blocks automated reading, so it is not included. LinkedIn has retired its salary pages, and its Power BI job postings in India rarely show pay (none of 60 Gurugram listings checked on 2 Nov 2026 did), so LinkedIn is not included either.
